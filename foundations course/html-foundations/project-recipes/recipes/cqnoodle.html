Recipe Description

Chongqing noodles is a general term given to a variety of noodle dishes originating from Chongqing, China. They all share the same flavour profile: fiercely spicy, mouth-numbing and super aromatic.
If you like Dan Dan Noodles, today’s recipe will definitely please your palate.


Ingredients

  • Chinese Chili Oil (Bought or homemade)
  • Ground Sichuan Pepper
  • Yellow Peas
  • Chinese Preserved Vegetable
  • Sweet Bean Sauce
  • Pork Intestine
  • Fresh Alkaline Noodles/ Gluten-Free Rice Noodles/ Dried Noodles
