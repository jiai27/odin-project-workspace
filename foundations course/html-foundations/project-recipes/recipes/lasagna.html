Recipe Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian casserole made by stacking wide, flat pasta sheets with alternating layers of rich meat or tomato sauce, creamy white sauce (like béchamel), and melted cheeses.
Baked until bubbly and golden, it is a hearty and deeply comforting baked pasta dish.


Ingredients

  • Meat
  • Spinach (any variation)
